1. Title & Problem Definition

Innov CRM: Streamlining B2B Sales Cycles by Eliminating Manual SLA Dispatch Gaps and Multi-User Stage Collisions

Duration: 8 Weeks
ICP: Mid-Market SaaS B2B Teams
Role: Product Lead (End-to-End Delivery)

2. Context & Background

Innov CRM is a dedicated B2B Sales Workflow system designed to accelerate pipelines for high-growth mid-market SaaS teams. As the lead Product Manager, I owned the opportunity workspace and pipeline progression engine.

This system demanded extremely tight cross-functional integration. I acted as the primary operational bridge:

  • Engineering Stakeholders: Partnered to design a lightweight client-side state synchronization model that prevents write clashes on heavy database workloads without dropping user queries.
  • Marketing Lead: Established webhook telemetry links between high-volume inbound capture forms and our automated lead scoring and assignment routes.
  • Sales Representatives: Audited day-to-day workflow patterns of 8 account executives (AEs) to understand lead conversion friction.

3. Problem Statement & JTBD

Sales agents spent up to 3 hours daily manually updating opportunity stages, tracking SLA follow-ups in external spreadsheets, and resolving database race conflicts when multiple reps attempted to edit or claim the exact same inbound deals simultaneously.

Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) Framing: "When I am a high-velocity mid-market sales representative dealing with 50+ active prospects, I want to drag deal cards to new stages and have follow-ups automatically dispatched without manual entries or overwriting another agent's active changes, so that I can dedicate my day to active pitching rather than database management."

4. User Persona & Research Methodology

I conducted structured shadowing sessions and 8 qualitative interviews with active sales teams. This real research highlighted how administrative delays directly leaked outbound pipeline momentum:

  • Overwrites notes and commits duplicate actions on shared outbound target lists due to UI desyncs.
Motivations & Goals
  • Wants to close larger deal sizes and achieve quota accelerators with minimal screen-switching.
  • Requires immediate, automated alerts for inbound client replies to secure <5 min response times.
  • Needs a fluid, mobile-first interface to update deal pipelines on-the-go during offsite clients visits.

Agents manually copy-pasted meeting logs and set next-step dates. High cognitive and administrative overhead, causing a 35% lag in updating sales statuses. SLA Automation Triggers: Auto-generating follow-up compliance tasks upon dragging deal cards.
Clashing browser tabs and overwrites during concurrent campaigns. No optimistic data versioning, resulting in duplicate task assignments and overwritten lead owners. Optimistic Locking Schema: Light client-side concurrency checks that flag writes instead of blocking DB connections.

5. Competitive Landscape & Alternatives Considered

Mid-market B2B teams typically rely on legacy tools like Salesforce or HubSpot. However, these systems require heavy customization, suffer from slow client-side rendering under massive lists, and lack out-of-the-box live write-clash prevention.

To solve write clashes without locking threads or slowing down the database, I evaluated two architectural pathways:

Alternative Option Pros Cons Tradeoff Verdict
Option A: Continuous WebSockets Connection Real-time bi-directional stage sync across all active agents. Extreme server-side connection overhead and high battery drain on mobile devices. Rejected for MVP due to infrastructure budget limits and poor performance on low-end hardware.
Option B: Client-side Optimistic locking Low bandwidth footprint; zero persistent connection overhead; fast database writes. Requires client to handle minor conflicts when version IDs mismatch on concurrent saves. Selected: Highly resilient, cost-effective, and guarantees write safety without locking database threads.

6. Solution, Wireframes & Accessibility Decisions

Our solution comprised a rapid-render drag-and-drop Kanban board paired with an event-driven task listener. To resolve database deadlocks under heavy concurrent updates, we chose an optimistic locking schema. Every deal card database record contains a version_id. When Vikram drags a card, the client sends this current ID. If a write conflict is detected (because another representative updated the record first), the UI gracefully displays a conflict resolution overlay.

UX and Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) Specifications

As a PM committed to inclusive design, I worked with engineering to implement strict accessibility standards in our wireframes:

  • High Contrast (WCAG 2.1 AA): All active status texts and deal priority indicators achieve a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against dark backgrounds, ensuring readability in direct sunlight for field sales reps.
  • Touch Target Size: Every drag handle and button in the mobile responsive layout has a minimum touch target area of 48px x 48px, preventing accidental click errors during fast-paced calls.
  • Screen Reader Accessibility: Added explicit aria-describedby tags describing deal values and pipeline stages, allowing visually impaired sales support staff to navigate the kanban via keyboard controls.

7. Go-To-Market & Business Model

To ensure smooth rollout and maximize commercial monetization, I formulated a dedicated GTM framework:

  • The Rollout Strategy: Delivered in 3 strict operational phases. Phase 1 (1 week) involved an internal sandbox alpha with 3 power agents to stress-test writing versions. Phase 2 (2 weeks) rolled out a partner beta with 35 active agents. Phase 3 marked General Availability with an interactive UI self-onboarding tour.
  • Business Model: Established a seat-based SaaS subscription tier starting at ₹1,200/seat/month, combined with custom API integration upsell tiers for larger enterprise sales platforms.

Failure & Iteration Story

The Bug: During the initial sandbox alpha, agents experienced frequent, frustrating conflict warning flags. Our initial client logic triggered database saves on every micro-drag event, causing instant version-clash overlays.

The Iteration: I redirected our engineering team to implement **client-side debouncing (350ms delay)**. The system now waits until a representative completely releases the card and it settles in its target stage slot before committing database version writes. The project required tight coordination with internal and external partners:

  • Engineering Leads: Managed system integration hooks to pull automatic git release tags and issue trackers into daily team briefing files.
  • UX Designers: Created clean, distraction-free visual layouts that group updates by context and priority.
  • Global Remote Teams: Gathered feedback from team members across multiple timezones to map collaboration pain points.

3. Problem Statement & JTBD

Distributed team members spent up to 1.5 hours daily parsing scattered chat threads or attending late-night status meetings just to align on daily priorities, resulting in severe decision blockages and decreased development focus.

Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) Framing: "When I am a remote engineer working in a different timezone than my core team, I want to receive a unified, summarized async briefing of daily updates, so that I can immediately align on critical blockers without attending live calls."

Failure & Iteration Story

The Bug: During the team beta rollout, our briefings became bloated and hard to read. Developers pasted massive code blocks and redundant notes, causing the daily digest to exceed 10 pages in length.

The Iteration: I directed engineering to implement a **strict character limit and semantic auto-summarization system**. Standup template inputs are now capped at 350 characters, and our system automatically filters out redundant logs while grouping active updates under a brief bulleted list. This reduced reading time to a crisp 3 minutes.

Shark Tank Live: Building a SEBI-Compliant Crowdfunding Overlay with UPI Escrow Rails

Duration: 10 Weeks
ICP: Indian Retail Investors & Startup Enthusiasts
Role: Fintech PM (Investments & Compliance Scoping)

2. Context & Background

Shark Tank Live is a structural PM case study focusing on retail crowdfunding localizations. As the lead Product Manager, I designed a television pitch show co-investing overlay that allows millions of retail viewers to pledge micro-equity (₹500+) during the broadcast, under strict Indian regulatory frameworks.

Balancing regulatory safety and direct payment UX was a highly collaborative effort:

  • SEBI Compliance Experts: Structured crowdsourcing frameworks to stay fully within equity investment limits and Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) rules.
  • Partner Bank Developers: Integrated direct **NPCI UPI AutoPay / Escrow mandate APIs** to securely capture and pledge transaction assets.
  • TV Broadcast Engineers: Synchronized live broadcast feeds with interactive overlay pings to ensure perfect alignment with pitching highlights.

3. Problem Statement & JTBD

Television viewers remained passive observers during pitch shows. Building a live co-investing system required resolving strict **SEBI equity crowdfunding limits** (restricting retail investments to verified SPVs) and designing a friction-free payment pledge flow that processes massive transaction spikes.

Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) Framing: "When I am a retail viewer passionate about an eco-friendly pitch on TV, I want to securely pledge ₹500 via a 5-second UPI transaction, so that I can hold fractional equity under a regulated investment vehicle without executing complex legal documents."

5. Competitive Landscape & Alternatives Considered

Standard angel syndicates mandate high investment floors, making start-up equity backing inaccessible to the majority of retail viewers.

To design the investment rails under Indian banking limits, I evaluated two transaction flows:

Alternative Option Pros Cons Tradeoff Verdict
Option A: Upfront Credit Card Pledging Instant checkouts; simple international integration. High merchant processing cuts; high card drop-off rates on mobile checkouts. Rejected: Excess fee cuts for micro-contributions.
Option B: UPI AutoPay Escrow Rails via NPCI APIs Funds are securely blocked in the user's account and debited only upon campaign success; zero fees. Requires handling massive concurrent mandate spikes during broadcast intervals. Selected: Unlocks frictionless, free micro-payments with complete investor safety.

6. Solution, Wireframes & Accessibility Decisions

I built an interactive TV overlay synchronized with real-time banking escrow mandates:

  • UPI AutoPay Escrow Rails: Pledged funds are held securely on the user's bank account via NPCI mandate blocks, and are only debited if the campaign achieves its target funding limit.
  • Automated SPV Equity Pools: Consolidates thousands of micro-investors into a single, SEBI-regulated SPV entity, preserving clean corporate tables for startup founders.
  • Live Synchronized Overlay: Renders interactive equity calculators directly on the smart TV / mobile app screen during active pitch sessions.

UX and Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) Specifications

To ensure absolute security and readability during live broadcast rushes:

  • SEBI Compliance Disclaimers: Risk disclaimers are written in clear, high-contrast text (> 4.7:1 ratio) to guarantee reading safety.
  • Tappable Calculators: Mobile equity investment sliders feature large, easily interactive handles (minimum 48px area).
  • Confirmation Sound Cues: Successful UPI transaction blocks trigger distinct sound and visual cues, providing positive confirmation to older users.

Failure & Iteration Story

The Bug: During our live debut, bank servers timed out as a massive concurrency wave of 250,000 simultaneous users tried to register UPI AutoPay blocks following a popular pitch conclusion, causing 45% of transaction attempts to fail.

The Iteration: I redirected our fintech engineering team to design a **transaction queue buffer system**. Instead of executing real-time bank handshakes immediately, users' pledge intents are securely validated client-side and grouped into a smart processing queue. The system registers the mandates asynchronously over a 15-minute window. This reduced server load by 80% and successfully brought transaction failure rates to zero.
